One field number for the limit: a 2310-step Antigravity conversation returned a ~18.79 MB trajectory, and delivery to it was refused with the quota-service error ("response exceeds size limit") while the quota itself was fine. Our workaround is a new window at ~1500 steps or once the trajectory passes ~12 MB, or /compact. Keeping the 16 MB default and not reading the whole conversation for a send both work for us. The actionable part is that the refusal message points at quota rather than at the body size.
